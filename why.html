02 / The Importance

Why Does It Matter?

Not everyone uses the internet in the same way. When websites aren't designed with different users in mind, some people can have a much harder time accessing information.

Barriers on the Web

Some websites are difficult to navigate or read, and others don't work well with assistive technology. This section will look at why those problems happen.

Why Designers Should Care

Accessibility shouldn't be something designers think about only after a website is finished. We'll look at why it matters throughout the design process.
