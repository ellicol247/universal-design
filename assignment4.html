Overview

Clash Royale is a real-time strategy mobile game released in 2016 by Supercell. It blends collectible card mechanics, tower defense, and MOBA-style gameplay. Players build decks and battle in short, fast-paced matches.

Gameplay

Battles take place in an arena with a King Tower and two Princess Towers. Players deploy cards using elixir to attack enemy towers while defending their own. Matches last up to three minutes, with overtime deciding ties.

Other Game Modes

Clash Royale includes 2v2 battles, seasonal challenges, draft modes, and special limited-time events. Clan Wars and Clan Wars 2 allow cooperative play across multiple decks.

Development History

Development began in 2014, evolving from a prototype called The Summoners. The game was designed for vertical mobile screens and launched globally in March 2016.

Monetization & Updates

Clash Royale uses a free-to-play model with microtransactions. Monthly seasons, balance changes, new cards, and features like Champions and Card Evolutions have been added over time.

Reception

Critics praised the game's strategic depth, fast matches, and mobile-friendly design. Some reviewers criticized its monetization and matchmaking.

Esports

Clash Royale has become a major mobile esport. The first official tournament took place in Helsinki in 2016, followed by the King's Cup later that year.

The first world championship, the 2017 Crown Championship, was held in London. Out of 27 million qualifying players, only 16 reached the finals, with Sergio Ramos winning the $150,000 prize.

Supercell launched the Clash Royale League (CRL) in 2018. The CRL World Finals reached over 140,000 peak viewers in 2024 and over 698,000 in 2025. The 2025 championship was won by Mohamed Light, marking his third world title.

Sales

Clash Royale generated over $1 billion in its first year and surpassed $4.9 billion in revenue by its tenth anniversary.
